Discovering Species, Discovering Bayes

Two Intuitive Learn Bayes Modules in JASP: Biodiversity Estimation

Leo (Ziyuan) Liu, Henrik Godmann, Eric-Jan Wagenmakers

Psychological Methods · University of Amsterdam

Read the full reportAbstractReferencesDownload PDF

Abstract

Frequentist statistics is dominant in education and research, but many students and even researchers misunderstand its key concepts (e.g., p-values and confidence intervals; Greenland et al., 2016). We suggest that this is partly because frequentist concepts are hard for beginners to comprehend. By contrast, the Bayesian framework may offer a more intuitive pathway into statistics. However, teaching Bayesian statistics to beginning students needs introductory-level materials. Therefore, we designed two JASP modules that introduce Bayesian statistics through an intuitive example of biodiversity estimation—estimating the number of different species on a fictional island. The static module introduces the fundamentals of Bayesian concepts in a straightforward way, while the full module extends the example by adding species abundance estimates. This project aims to bring Bayesian statistics to a younger audience, improving Bayesian literacy and promoting reform in statistical education.

Keywords: Bayesian Statistics, Statistical Education, JASP, Biodiversity Estimation

Module availability. Both modules, Biodiversity Estimation (Static) and Biodiversity Estimation (Full), will be available in the next version of JASP under “Learn Bayes”.
